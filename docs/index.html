            How Toonlight works

            1. Pick a character. Every visit starts a new chat, and your past chats are in the Chats list on the left (synced to your account when you're signed in).
            2. Choose what to practice: a quiz (Easy, Medium or Hard), a mock interview, a new language, or just chat.
            3. Press the paperclip to upload notes or slides. They'll study it, answer questions from it and quiz you on it.
            4. Type or press the mic to talk. Switch to Voice to hear them answer out loud.
            5. Sign in to use Agent mode: give a goal and watch them work through it step by step. They can research, read links, check weather and news, do exact math, draw pictures, make files, set reminders and write messages. Anything that acts for you waits for your button press.
            6. Open the Notebook to tell them about you, or give a character your own 3D model.
