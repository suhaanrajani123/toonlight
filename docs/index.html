      How Toonlight works

      1. Pick a character on the left.
      2. Choose what to practice: a quiz, a mock interview, a new language, or just chat.
      3. Type or press the mic to talk. They answer out loud.
      4. Open the Notebook to tell them about you, or give a character your own 3D model.
