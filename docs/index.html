            How Toonlight works

            • Pick a character from the faces at the top. Each one has their own voice and personality.
            • Choose what to do: just chat, hand a task to the Agent, get quizzed, practise an interview or practise a language.
            • Quiz me on anything: type a topic, paste your notes, or press the paperclip to upload a PDF, Word file or slides. They read it first, then ask.
            • Agent (needs a free account) works through a goal step by step: research, weather and news, exact math, pictures, files, messages and reminders. Connect Google Calendar in the Notebook and events go straight in.
            • Talk out loud: press the mic to speak, and switch to Voice to hear replies.
            • Your chats are saved in the list on the left, and follow you to other devices when you're signed in.
