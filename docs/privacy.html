Toonlight privacy policy

Last updated October 22, 2026. Toonlight is a free, non-commercial student project by Suhaan Rajani.

What Toonlight stores

In your browser only: your settings, the Notebook (name, goals, saved facts, contacts, reminders), documents you upload, pictures the agent makes, and chats while you're signed out. They never leave your device unless noted below, and you can delete them in the Notebook or by clearing your browser data.

With your account (only if you sign in): your email address (and your name and profile picture if you sign in with Google, GitHub or Facebook) and your saved chats, so they appear on your other devices. They're stored with Supabase and protected so that only you can read them.

Google Calendar (only if you connect it)

If you connect Google Calendar in the Notebook, Google asks you to allow one permission: to see, create, change and delete events in your calendars. Toonlight uses it only to add the reminders and events you ask for, and to remove one when you press Undo. It doesn't read your other events. Google's sign-in key for this stays in your browser tab for about an hour; it's never sent to Toonlight's server or saved to your account. You can disconnect in the Notebook, or remove Toonlight's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Toonlight's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

What is sent to the AI

To answer you, your recent messages, your Notebook name, goals and facts, and short passages from a document you're studying are sent to Toonlight's server on Cloudflare and to the AI model (Meta Llama on Cloudflare Workers AI). The server doesn't keep a copy. In Agent mode, the agent may look things up on public services (for example Wikipedia, news feeds, weather and map services) using only the words of your request.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking or analytics, no selling or sharing of your data. Phone numbers and email addresses in your Contacts are never sent to the AI or to any server.

Deleting your data

Chats: delete them one by one in the Chats sidebar, and they're removed from your account too. Everything stored in your browser: use "Forget everything" in the Notebook or clear your browser data. To delete your whole account and everything stored with it, including if you signed in with Facebook, Google or GitHub, contact the developer (below) from the email address you signed in with, and it will be deleted within 7 days.
